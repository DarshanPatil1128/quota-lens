How it works

1 · Score every deal

Each open deal starts with its stage’s historical win rate. Deals that have sat in a stage for more than 45 days get a haircut, because stalled deals close less often.

2 · Account for timing

Winning isn’t enough — the deal has to land this quarter. Each deal carries a slip risk that is highest in early stages and smallest at Commit.

3 · Simulate the quarter

For each simulated quarter, every deal is randomly won or lost using its probability. Adding the wins to closed-won gives one possible outcome; repeating thousands of times gives the full distribution.

4 · Find the swing deals

Comparing quarters where a deal was won with quarters where it wasn’t shows how much each deal changes the odds — so a team knows where to spend executive time.

CSV columns: deal_id, deal, account, owner, stage, amount, close_date, days_in_stage. Stages: Discover, Qualify, Propose, Negotiate, Commit. Sample data is synthetic and for demonstration only.
